About College

, located in Valamboor near Perinthalmanna in Kerala's Malappuram district, is a minority-status private self-financing institution established in 2010. Affiliated with the University of Calicut and recognized by the Government of Kerala, the college was founded with the core objective of uplifting minority and marginalized communities through value-based higher education. It offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programs—spanning commerce, management, humanities, IT, and sciences like Microbiology and Food Technology—while focusing on academic rigor, moral soundness, and professional competence to shape socially responsible citizens.
